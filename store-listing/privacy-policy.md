# AirZip Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-21

AirZip does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, usage data, or telemetry.

AirZip does not connect to the internet. It reads and writes only the files you explicitly open or extract, on your own device.

AirZip requests no permissions beyond running as a desktop app (`runFullTrust`) and does not access your camera, microphone, location, contacts, or any other protected resource.

## The AirZip website

The above describes the AirZip application. The project website at
https://airzip.vercel.app uses Vercel Web Analytics to count page views and Vercel Speed
Insights to measure page load performance. Both are cookieless, store no personal data,
and do not track visitors across other sites. Nothing on the website is linked to your use
of the app.
